# Contributing to Historical Book AI Translator

First off, thank you for taking an interest in this project!

Historical Book AI Translator is an open-source project dedicated to preserving historical knowledge through AI-assisted transcription, translation, and research.

I'm currently developing and testing this project independently, using older computer hardware and the resources available to me. Development is ongoing, and I welcome contributions from people with all levels of experience.

You don't have to be an expert programmer or historian to help!

## Our Mission

The initial goal is to make historical books more accessible by converting scanned pages into readable, searchable, and translated text.

Our first source is William Gilbert's *De Magnete* (1600), translated from Latin into English using Python, AMD Lemonade, and vision-language AI models.

The long-term vision is much larger: building historically grounded AI agents representing specific periods of history, with the eventual possibility of interactive historical environments and virtual reality.

Before any of that, we need reliable historical source material.

**Accuracy, transparency, and preservation come first.**

## Ways You Can Contribute

### 1. Historical Transcription and Translation

- Review AI-generated Latin transcriptions.
- Correct OCR and transcription mistakes.
- Compare translations against original scanned pages.
- Help preserve historical spelling and terminology.
- Identify passages requiring human verification.
- Suggest improvements to translation accuracy.

### 2. Programming and AI Development

- Improve Python scripts and error handling.
- Optimize local AI processing for limited hardware.
- Improve support for AMD Lemonade and compatible AI backends.
- Develop better page-processing and correction workflows.
- Help improve performance, logging, and recovery after interrupted runs.
- Test different vision-language models.

### 3. Historical Research

- Recommend historical books and manuscripts.
- Help verify publication dates and source editions.
- Identify public-domain historical materials.
- Suggest additional languages.
- Help organize historical sources by time period.

### 4. Future Development Ideas

Ideas are welcome for:

- Period-specific historical AI agents.
- Historically restricted knowledge datasets.
- Interactive conversations with historical scholars.
- Historical simulations and educational tools.
- Virtual reality environments.
- New translation and transcription features.

These are long-term research goals, not completed features.

## Historical Accuracy Guidelines

This project deals with original historical sources, so accuracy matters.

When contributing:

1. Preserve the meaning of the original text.
2. Do not silently invent or replace unreadable words.
3. Mark uncertain transcriptions clearly.
4. Distinguish original text from modernized spelling.
5. Keep translations faithful to the historical source.
6. Provide source links and page references when possible.
7. Clearly identify AI-generated or unverified material.
8. Avoid introducing modern scientific knowledge into historical interpretations.

The goal is to understand what historical authors actually wrote and believed, even when their explanations differ from modern science.

## How to Submit Contributions

For suggestions, questions, or ideas, open a GitHub Discussion.

For bugs, transcription mistakes, or specific improvements, open a GitHub Issue.

For code or documentation contributions:

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your changes.
3. Make and test your changes.
4. Explain what you changed and why.
5. Submit a Pull Request.

For historical corrections, please include the book title, edition or source link, and page number whenever possible.

## Source Materials and Copyright

Only contribute historical scans, transcriptions, and translations when you have the necessary rights or permission to share them.

A historical work being old does not automatically mean every modern scan, edition, or translation can be redistributed without restrictions.

Please include source attribution and relevant rights information.

## Security and Privacy

Never upload API keys, passwords, authentication tokens, or other private credentials.

Use environment variables for credentials and remove sensitive information from logs before sharing them.
